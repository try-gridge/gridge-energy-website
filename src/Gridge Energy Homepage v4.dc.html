03 PRODUCT
The Panel

Calm intelligence. Wired in.

One panel. No app clutter. Gridge sits at your distribution board and identifies appliances from their electrical signature - passively, with no extra sensors. We don't add sensors to old hardware. We replace it entirely.

It sees every circuit

Continuous per-circuit monitoring, with appliances identified by NILM from their electrical fingerprint - no additional sensors.

It acts on its own

Scheduling, protection and automation run from the panel itself. No subscription. Control stays in your home.

It is ready for the grid ahead

Gridge panels respond to grid signals in real time, enabling demand response and future energy market participation.

07 ENGINEERING
Engineering

Built from the grid up, not bolted on.

Solid-state hardware handles the physical switching and sensing at the panel. The same AI that schedules appliances and flags failures runs locally on that hardware, so protection and control keep working with no internet at all - cloud connectivity is optional to core operation.

01 - Power

Designed for 170-270V

No mechanical relays - solid-state switching handles India's full voltage range.

02 - Sensing

No extra hardware

Every circuit monitored continuously - no additional sensors needed.

03 - Intelligence

Decisions happen at home

Core intelligence runs on the panel - smart and safe even offline.

04 - Connectivity
