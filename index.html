The Problem

India's homes consume 30% of national electricity — with zero visibility.

The distribution board in every Indian home is a mechanical relic of the 1920s. It cannot measure, communicate, or respond. This silence costs India billions every year in avoidable infrastructure spending.

01 — The dumb board

A century without innovation

Every Indian home runs on 100-year-old mechanical technology. No data, no control, no intelligence whatsoever.

100 yrs
Without meaningful innovation
02 — The grid crisis

The peak CAPEX spiral

India sizes its entire power infrastructure for a 3–4 hour daily peak. DISCOMs spend billions on CAPEX that sits idle 20+ hours a day.

$15B+
Avoidable grid infrastructure cost
03 — The EV crisis

The electric vehicle time bomb

One EV fast charger adds the load of 50–70 households — threatening infrastructure built for a completely different era.

About Gridge

Calm intelligence.
Wired in.

One panel. No app clutter. Gridge sits at your distribution board and reads the electrical signature of every device — passively, continuously, precisely.

We don't add sensors to old hardware. We replace it entirely — with something built for the present and ready for what's next.

Appliance Detection
Identifies every appliance by its unique electrical fingerprint — AC, fridge, water heater — all mapped automatically.
Circuit-Level Insight
Real-time visibility into every circuit. Spot anomalies, predict failures, and optimise load distribution before problems become bills.
Silent Automation
Schedule, protect, and automate — from the panel itself. No subscription required. Control stays in your home.
Grid-Responsive by Design
Gridge panels respond to grid signals in real time — enabling demand response and future energy market participation.
